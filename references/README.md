## 2. Regulatory & Data Sources

### Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) Standard
- **Authority:** Central Pollution Control Board, Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, Government of India.
- **Key Document:** *National Air Quality Index (NAQI) Report* (2014, implemented 2015).
- **Core Standard:** Six AQI categories:
  - Good (0–50)
  - Satisfactory (51–100)
  - Moderate (101–200)
  - Poor (201–300)
  - Very Poor (301–400)
  - Severe (401–500+)
- **Criteria Pollutants:** $PM_{2.5}, PM_{10}, NO_2, NH_3, SO_2, CO, O_3, \text{Pb}$.

### Primary Dataset
- **Author / Curator:** Rohan Rao (Kaggle).
- **Dataset:** *Air Quality Data in India (2015–2020)*.
- **Origin:** Continuous Ambient Air Quality Monitoring Stations (CAAQMS) under the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) of India.
- **License / URL:** [https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rohanrao/air-quality-data-in-india](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rohanrao/air-quality-data-in-india)
